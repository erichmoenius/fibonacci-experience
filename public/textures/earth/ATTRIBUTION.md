# Earth textures — Theme 4 / OUR WORLD

Planet textures by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, based on NASA imagery/elevation data.
Source collection: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

All five local maps are 4096 x 2048 derivatives of the aligned 8192 x 4096
equirectangular Earth set. Original filenames, download sources, modifications,
and final SHA-256 hashes are recorded in manifest.json. The normal map was
retrieved from the Wikimedia Commons mirror of the same Solar System Scope work:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_8k_earth_normal_map.tif
No texture is upscaled. Data maps are stored losslessly after downsampling.

Retain this attribution with distributions. CC BY permits adaptation and
commercial redistribution with attribution and indication of modifications.
NASA is the underlying data source; the adapted Solar System Scope maps are
credited under CC BY rather than described as unmodified public-domain NASA data.

The collection uses a static seasonal surface composite and static sample cloud
coverage, with enhanced source saturation. This is an artistic Earth depiction,
not current weather, current city light observations, or a scientific simulation.
